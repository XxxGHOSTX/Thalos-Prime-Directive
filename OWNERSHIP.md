# Ownership and Authorship Status

This document records repository attribution and the current evidence limits. It is not a legal opinion, a complete chain-of-title certificate, or a transfer or grant of rights.

## Attribution

Repository notices identify Tony Ray Macier III as the project author and copyright claimant. Those notices record the project's stated attribution; they do not, by themselves, independently establish authorship or exclusive ownership of every file, contribution, dependency, or related work in the Thalos Prime project family.

Any ownership claim should be limited to original material personally authored by the claimant or rights validly acquired through an applicable written agreement or other legally effective transfer.

## Third-party and contributor material

This declaration does not claim ownership of:

- Third-party libraries, frameworks, tools, models, datasets, or other dependencies;
- Material authored by other contributors, contractors, employees, or collaborators unless the relevant rights were validly assigned or licensed;
- Material distributed under an applicable repository, package, or file-level open-source license; or
- Pre-existing works incorporated into the project.

The project family contains different repository-level licensing positions, including MIT, Apache-2.0, and custom proprietary notices. Each repository and file must be assessed under the license and provenance that actually apply to it. This document does not amend, revoke, override, or replace any existing license or third-party grant.

## Chain-of-title evidence required

Before asserting exclusive ownership of a particular component, retain and review:

1. The relevant repository and file history, including authorship and contribution records;
2. Contractor, employee, collaboration, and work-made-for-hire agreements where applicable;
3. Written assignments, licenses, contributor agreements, or other transfers covering the specific material;
4. Third-party dependency and dataset licenses; and
5. Any applicable prior-publication or public-disclosure records.

A contributor's acknowledgment or a copyright header is not a substitute for an effective assignment where one is required.

## Publication, registration, patent, and trademark status

This document does not assert that all project materials are unpublished, that a copyright registration has been filed or granted, that a patent application exists or is pending, or that the THALOS PRIME mark is cleared or registered. Those matters require separate verification against the relevant official records. The first-publication date and complete registration/filing status have not been established by this declaration.

## Scope and effect

This document is an informational correction to avoid overstating repository evidence. It does not determine legal ownership, inventorship, patentability, infringement, royalty entitlement, or trademark availability. Obtain qualified legal review before relying on any ownership, filing, licensing, or enforcement position.
